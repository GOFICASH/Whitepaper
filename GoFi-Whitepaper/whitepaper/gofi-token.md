# The GOFI token

GOFI is a membership token: owning it unlocks real services every month. It launches in a later phase; nothing in this section is live yet.

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Total supply | 1,000,000,000 GOFI |
| Time to unlock benefits | 5 days of staking or holding |
| Revenue to buyback and burn | 25% of monthly platform revenue |
| Burn target | 450,000,000 GOFI (45% of supply) |
| Supply after the burn target | 550,000,000 GOFI |

## What GOFI does

* **Pay with it.** Buy eSIMs, virtual numbers and card top-ups with GOFI.
* **Stake it.** Staking (or holding) for 5 days unlocks the membership tier that matches the balance.
* **Benefit from usage.** Each month, a quarter of platform revenue buys GOFI on the market and burns it, until 450 million tokens are gone.

## The flywheel

1. People buy, stake or spend GOFI.
2. Their tier unlocks discounts, free plans and card benefits each month.
3. They use eSIMs, numbers and the card more.
4. More usage means more revenue.
5. A quarter of that revenue buys back and burns GOFI, shrinking supply toward 550 million.
6. The loop starts again at step 1.

The buyback is funded by service revenue, not by new token sales, so it only grows when real usage grows.
