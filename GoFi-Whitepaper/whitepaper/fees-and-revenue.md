# Fees and revenue

GoFi earns a margin on every order. Revenue comes from usage, not from token sales, so the business stands on its own before the token launches.

| Product | User pays | How GoFi earns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| eSIM data | The plan price | Margin over wholesale data cost. eSIM resellers typically keep 30–55% gross margin, and top-ups add 20–30% more revenue per customer. |
| Virtual numbers | Rental and renewal price | Margin on each rental and renewal; premium countries carry a higher margin. |
| Prepaid card | $0 to issue, 1% per top-up, 1.5% FX on non-USD spend | Top-up and FX fees, plus a share of interchange from the issuer. |
| Network fees | Paid by the user in their wallet | None. Shown at checkout. |

The card fees above are the launch schedule. GoFi plans to cut them toward the lowest in the market as card volume grows (see [Roadmap](roadmap.md)).

The three products feed each other. A traveller who buys a data plan is the same person who needs a number to sign up and a card to pay. One checkout sells all three to the same user.
