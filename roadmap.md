# Roadmap

GoFi ships what people pay for first, then adds the token on top. No dates are set beyond the current phase.

| Phase       | What ships                                                                                                                                                                      | Status               |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Now         | Web app, USDC and USDG checkout, eSIM plans, virtual numbers, virtual prepaid card                                                                                              | Live                 |
| Next        | Mobile apps, Apple Pay and Google Pay (issuer talks under way, including Rain), self-custodial wallet, lower top-up and FX fees, more tokens and chains, a second eSIM supplier | In talks and planned |
| Later       | Cheapest physical card, gift cards, x402 payments for AI agents, partner API, airport lounge discounts and card holder offers                                                   | Planned              |
| Token phase | GOFI launch, membership tiers, paying in GOFI, monthly buyback and burn                                                                                                         | Planned              |
