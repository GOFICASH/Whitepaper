# Abstract

## Abstract

GoFi Cash turns the stablecoins in your wallet into things you can use: travel eSIM data, virtual phone numbers and a prepaid card. You connect a wallet, pick what you need and pay. No bank account, no exchange account, and GoFi never holds your crypto.

The product is live at [gofi.cash](https://www.gofi.cash/) and takes USDC on Solana and USDG on Robinhood Chain. GoFi earns a margin on every order, so the business does not depend on a token.

The GOFI token, planned for a later phase, adds a membership layer on top. Holders and stakers unlock discounts and free plans, can pay for services in GOFI, and 25% of monthly revenue goes to buying back and burning the token.
