# How it works

![How an order flows: your wallet pays, GoFi routes the order, the provider delivers](.gitbook/assets/how-it-works.png)

The user signs one payment from their own wallet. GoFi prices the order, routes it to a licensed provider, and the result appears in the GoFi dashboard within minutes. GoFi builds on existing suppliers and card issuers instead of becoming a telecom or a bank, which keeps launch fast and lets it add providers market by market.

| Step | Who | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Your wallet | Pays in USDC on Solana or USDG on Robinhood |
| 2 | GoFi checkout | Shows the price in your token, routes the order, never holds your keys |
| 3 | eSIM suppliers | Deliver the eSIM QR code to your dashboard |
| 3 | Number providers | Deliver the number to your dashboard |
| 3 | Card issuer | Loads and holds the card balance |
