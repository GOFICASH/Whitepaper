# Membership tiers

The more GOFI a user stakes or holds, the more they get back each month. Benefits start after 5 days. If a balance drops below a tier, the user moves to the tier they still qualify for.

| Tier        | Hold      | eSIM benefit                  | Card benefit                  | Numbers benefit       |
| ----------- | --------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Access      | 100K GOFI | 15% off plans                 | 5% cashback                   | Holder pricing        |
| Plus        | 500K GOFI | 30% off + 1 free plan a month | 10% cashback                  | 1 free every 6 months |
| Pro         | 1M GOFI   | 45% off + 1 free plan a month | 15% cashback + card credits   | 2 free every 6 months |
| Global Pass | 5M GOFI   | 2 free plans a month          | 25% cashback + 5 card credits | 2 free every 6 months |
